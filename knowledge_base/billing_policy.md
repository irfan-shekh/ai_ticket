# Billing & Payment Policies

## 1. Subscription Billing & Automatic Renewals
- Subscriptions automatically renew at the beginning of each billing cycle (monthly or annual).
- Invoices are generated automatically and sent to the billing contact email on record within 24 hours of payment.
- To change subscription plans, navigate to Account Settings > Billing > Subscription Plan.

## 2. Duplicate or Erroneous Charges
- If a customer observes a double charge on their bank statement:
  1. Verify whether one of the charges is a temporary authorization hold (which clears within 3-5 business days).
  2. If two settled transactions appear with identical amounts and timestamps, request the transaction reference ID.
  3. Support agents will issue an immediate refund for duplicate settled charges. Refunds reflect on bank statements within 5-10 business days.

## 3. Updating Payment Methods & Invoices
- Customers can update credit card or debit card details under Settings > Payment Methods.
- Past invoices can be downloaded in PDF format directly from the Customer Portal Billing tab.
- We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and corporate ACH transfers.
