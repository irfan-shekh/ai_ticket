# Customer Complaints Handling & Dispute Redressal

## 1. Complaint Intake & Senior Review SLA
- All complaints regarding representative conduct, delayed deliveries, or poor service quality are assigned to Tier-2 Customer Relations.
- A dedicated Customer Care Manager reviews the incident within 12 business hours.
- Customers receive formal written follow-up outlining root causes and corrective measures taken.

## 2. Delayed Delivery Compensation
- If a package is delayed beyond the guaranteed carrier shipping window by more than 3 business days:
  1. The full shipping fee is refunded to the customer.
  2. A 15% courtesy credit is applied toward the customer's next order.

## 3. Damaged Service Experience Redressal
- When a customer is dissatisfied with frontline support or service interactions:
  1. Ticket is flagged as 'needs_review' with priority escalation.
  2. A senior supervisor contacts the customer to address concerns directly.
