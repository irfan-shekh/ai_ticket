# Returns, Refunds & Replacement Policies

## 1. 30-Day Return Window
- Customers are eligible for a full refund or exchange within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- Items must be in their original packaging, unopened or lightly inspected, with all original accessories included.

## 2. Damaged or Defective Items on Delivery
- If an item arrives broken, damaged, or defective:
  1. The customer does not pay return shipping fees.
  2. Customers should provide a photo of the package damage and invoice number.
  3. A prepaid return shipping label will be generated and emailed within 4 business hours.
  4. Once carrier scan confirmation occurs, a replacement or full refund is initiated immediately.

## 3. Refund Processing Timelines
- Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method.
- Credit card refunds: 5-7 business days.
- Debit card refunds: 7-10 business days.
- Store credit: Instantaneous upon warehouse check-in.
